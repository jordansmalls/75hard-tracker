The rules of 75 Hard

75 Hard is a mental toughness program created by Andy Frisella. For 75 days in a row, you do every one of these:

  1. 1

    Follow a diet

    Any diet you choose, as long as it’s structured. No cheat meals.

  2. 2

    No alcohol

    Not a single drink for all 75 days.

  3. 3

    Two 45-minute workouts

    Every day, and one of them has to be outdoors, whatever the weather.

  4. 4

    Drink a gallon of water

    That’s 128 ounces, or about 3.8 liters.

  5. 5

    Read 10 pages

    Nonfiction, from an actual book. Audiobooks don’t count.

  6. 6

    Take a progress picture

    One every day, so you can see the change for yourself.

Miss anything, start over

Skip a task on any day and you go back to Day 1. No substitutions, no exceptions.

What the app does

  • Today’s checklist

    All seven tasks on one screen. One tap checks a task off, and a ring shows how much of today is done.

  • All 75 days at a glance

    Seventy-five empty circles that fill in with a check as you finish each day. Tap any day to look back at it.

  • Honest about missed days

    If a day wasn’t finished, you’ll know. Fill in what you forgot to log, or start over from Day 1 in one tap.

  • Insights that add up

    Pages read, gallons drunk, workouts and hours trained, your streak, your usual reading time, and which task you always save for last.

  • Water and reading, logged as you go

    Tap in ounces through the day and the gallon checks itself off. Log the book and pages, and keep a list of everything you read.

  • Progress photos, side by side

    Snap the day’s photo from the checklist, then drag a slider to compare Day 1 with today.

  • Already partway in?

    Pick the day you really started and pick up right where you are.

  • Made for your phone

    Designed to feel at home on iPhone, in light or dark mode. Add it to your home screen and go.
